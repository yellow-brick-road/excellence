# About Excellence

> Last updated: 2026-02-20

## What Is Excellence?

Excellence is a **cross-cutting function** created within the Helix organizational model to **improve productivity, efficiency, and autonomy** across Digital Product & Technology (DP&T) teams.

It is not a product vertical, not a platform, not a support team, not a control body.

**It is an accelerator and force multiplier** — improving *how* software gets built, not *what* software gets built.

### Objective

> Scale productivity and engagement in DP&T, maximizing return on investment.

### Expected Outcomes

- Measurable uplift in AI adoption
- Increased productivity
- Higher agile maturity
- Better alignment across all teams

---

## What Excellence Does (In Scope)

### 1. Best Practices & Standards
- Define, promote, and scale technical and delivery best practices
- Structured governance without bureaucracy
- Prevent teams from reinventing the wheel

### 2. Enablement & Mentoring
- Accompany teams to adopt practices — help, don't impose
- Spot workflow issues early and help teams fix them

### 3. Innovation, Research & Testing
- New technologies, processes, tools
- Tooling automation, AI adoption, productivity improvements

### 4. Shared Services
- Technical services common across verticals (no product ownership)
- Examples cited: Authentication, Messaging services

### 5. Community & Collaboration
- Foster collaboration among internal and external teams
- Interoperability between excellence teams

### 6. Observability & Productivity KPIs
- Monitor and communicate performance
- Technical and delivery KPIs (not business KPIs)

---

## What Excellence Does NOT Do (Out of Scope)

- ❌ Own product backlogs
- ❌ Define business priorities
- ❌ Build features for verticals
- ❌ Act as an approval gate or checkpoint
- ❌ Add bureaucracy
- ❌ Become a bottleneck

> "Excellence will not own the backlogs and will not act as an approval bottleneck. We don't want to improve delivery times by adding bureaucracy."

---

## Types of Excellence

| Type | Focus |
|------|-------|
| **Operational / Process** | Process improvement, standardization, efficiency, metrics, reporting |
| **Technology** | Technical best practices, architecture, QA, guilds, tooling, AI, scalability |
| **Business / Customer** | Customer experience, service quality, business-tech alignment |

---

## Operating Model

- Does NOT command product teams
- Collaborates with them, embeds temporarily, or works as a **guild**
- Detects common problems across teams and proposes reusable solutions
- Defines **guides, frameworks, and standards** — not product features
- Helps teams go faster with less friction

### Guiding Principles

- Culture before processes
- Team autonomy
- Reduce dependencies
- Flexible guides based on real cases
- Continuous system improvement

---

## Organization

- **6 Excellence teams**, each with a lead
- Details to be shared in functional all-hands sessions
- Part of the full Helix deployment

### Timeline

- Progressive implementation
- Operational start: **June 2026**
