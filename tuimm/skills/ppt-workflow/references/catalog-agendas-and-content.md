## Slide 39 — Agenda: 6 Items with Navy Sidebar

**Layout:** `Content Slide 15`
**Visual:** Left half has light blue background with 6 numbered white topic bars. Right side has dark navy vertical sidebar with "AGENDA" header. Meeting name field at top.
**Use for:** Standard meeting agenda up to 6 items.

**Editable shapes:**

| Shape | Name | Content to fill |
|-------|------|----------------|
| Title | `Title 2` (PH 0) | Meeting name (optional) |
| Sidebar | `Rectangle: Rounded Corners 5` | "AGENDA" (or custom header) |
| Topic 1 | `Rectangle: Rounded Corners 6` (id=7) | First agenda item |
| Number 1 | `Oval 7` (id=8) | "1" |
| Topic 2 | `Rectangle: Rounded Corners 6` (id=9) | Second agenda item |
| Number 2 | `Oval 11` (id=10) | "2" |
| Topic 3 | `Rectangle: Rounded Corners 6` (id=12) | Third agenda item |
| Number 3 | `Oval 11` (id=11) | "3" |
| Topic 4 | `Rectangle: Rounded Corners 6` (id=16) | Fourth agenda item |
| Number 4 | `Oval 14` (id=15) | "4" |
| Topic 5 | `Rectangle: Rounded Corners 6` (id=14) | Fifth agenda item |
| Number 5 | `Oval 11` (id=13) | "5" |
| Topic 6 | `Rectangle: Rounded Corners 6` (id=17) | Sixth agenda item |
| Number 6 | `Oval 11` (id=18) | "6" |

**Pattern:** Ovals contain the number, Rounded Corners rectangles contain the topic text. Match by shape_id.
